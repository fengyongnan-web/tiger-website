About Us

Company Profile

TIGER International Trade Co Ltd is a newly established company focused on cross-border trade services, headquartered in China with business covering Korea and multiple countries worldwide.

We provide global sourcing and supply chain integration for electronic components, industrial equipment, consumer electronics and raw materials, helping customers efficiently connect with quality suppliers and reduce procurement costs.

Our Advantages

  • Professional procurement team familiar with China-Korea markets and supply chain resources
  • Strict quality inspection to ensure every shipment meets customer requirements
  • Flexible trade modes: general trade, sample procurement, custom sourcing
  • Efficient logistics and customs clearance with full tracking
  • Bilingual communication in Chinese, English and Korean

Our Philosophy

Integrity first, professionalism as foundation, efficiency as priority. We believe every trade cooperation is the beginning of long-term trust. TIGER is ready to work with global partners for mutual success.
